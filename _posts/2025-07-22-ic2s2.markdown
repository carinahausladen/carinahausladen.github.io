# IC2S2 2025 in Norrköping

I attended **IC2S2 2025** in Norrköping, one of the key international conferences in computational social science. 


I presented my work in a session chaired by **Martin Hilbert**, alongside a strong lineup of papers on social influence, institutions, and collective action:

- *Social influence and behavioral contagion: A Reddit bot field experiment*  
- *Celebrity counterspeech reduces hate content in the Nigerian Twitter*  
- *Institutional preferences in the laboratory*  
- **Identifying latent intentions via inverse reinforcement learning in repeated linear public good games** (my presentation)  
- *Reasoning within and between collective action problems*

My presentation focused on how inverse reinforcement learning can be used to recover latent motivations in repeated public good interactions, contributing to the broader discussion on institutional behavior and social preferences.
