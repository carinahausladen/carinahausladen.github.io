```yaml
- title: "Beyond the Townhall: Spatial Anchoring and LLM Agents for Scalable Participatory Urban Planning"
  authors: C. I. Hausladen, J. A. Sánchez-Vaquerizo, M. Siebenmann, A. Capozzi, S. Mahajan, D. Helbing
  venue: arXiv preprint
  venue_short: arXiv 2026
  year: 2026
  image: /images/lausanne-nzz.jpg
  url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16348
  arxiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16348
  press: https://sustainableswitzerland.ch/artikel/eth/jenseits-der-gemeindeversammlung-wie-visualisierung-die-schweizer-demokratie-staerken-kann-id.4668
  abstract: >
    We embed sustainability projects in a navigable digital twin: citizens take a guided virtual
    walkthrough with audio narration that uses spatial anchoring (the method of loci) to support
    recall, aided by two purpose-built LLM assistants — one giving source-grounded clarifications,
    the other facilitating reflective discussion. In a randomized experiment (N = 195) against
    static visualizations and text consultations, the immersive, spatially anchored presentation
    improved information recall, shifted attention from individual inconveniences toward collective
    benefits, and elicited more constructive, solution-focused feedback.
```

### A Virtual Reality Experiment to Study Pedestrian Perception (Sci. Reports 2024)
```yaml
- title: A Virtual Reality Experiment to Study Pedestrian Perception of Future Street Scenarios
  authors: J. Argota Sánchez-Vaquerizo, C. I. Hausladen, S. Mahajan, M. Matter, M. Siebenmann, M. van Eggermond, D. Helbing
  venue: Scientific Reports
  venue_short: Sci. Reports
  year: 2024
  image: /images/VR4.jpg
  url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-55073-x
  html: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-55073-x
  citations: 28
  abstract: >
    Flexible street layouts were built in immersive VR, and participants crossed streets under
    varied designs, times of day, and communication strategies while we measured self-reported,
    behavioural, and physiological responses. People preferred familiar solutions; unconventional
    layouts felt less safe and raised stress — suggesting new street designs must be developed
    inclusively and democratically.
```
